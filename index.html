H3 Max is the speed-optimized build of MiniMax H3, the open-weight multimodal video model. It turns a prompt or an image into a 5-second 768p clip in roughly three seconds — faster than the video plays. h3max.info collects the official links, APIs, open weights, guides and live projects so you can start building in minutes.

What is H3 Max?

Three names show up everywhere and they are not the same thing. Here is the short version.

Official · open weights

MiniMax H3

MiniMax's next-generation general video model. Takes text, images, video and audio in one request and returns 5–15 seconds of video at 24 fps with native stereo audio. Announced July 31, 2026 (API), weights published August 3 under the MiniMax H3 Community License.

  • Generation, referencing and editing in a single model
  • Hosted API up to 2K; open checkpoints (FL2VA / Ref2VA) generate 768p natively
  • Self-hostable on a single high-end GPU
fal.ai · speed build

H3 Max

Jointly released by MiniMax and fal.ai on August 29, 2026. fal post-trained MiniMax H3 for prompt adherence and visual quality and co-designed the inference stack for speed: a 5-second 768p clip in about three seconds, dozens of times the throughput of the official endpoint.

  • Text-to-video, image-to-video and reference-to-video endpoints
  • 480p and 768p output, API only (no open weights)
  • Fast enough for real-time and interactive video streams
fal.ai · fastest tier

H3 Max Turbo

A further-accelerated variant of H3 Max served at minimax/h3-max-turbo. Same 480p/768p outputs and the lowest per-second price in the family. fal has not published architectural details on how it differs from H3 Max.

  • Text-to-video and image-to-video
  • Best fit for high-volume batch generation and live use
  • Pricing and limits change often — check fal.ai before you budget
